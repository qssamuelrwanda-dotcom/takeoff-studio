Features

Everything a take-off needs, in one screen

Built by quantity surveyors for quantity surveyors. Every element you draw becomes measured quantities straight away, and each one is linked back to the model so you can check where a number came from.

Trace PDFs

Import a drawing, set its scale from any known dimension, and trace walls, slabs and rooms straight over it.

Live 3D model

See the building grow as you draw it. Hide levels or element types, and click in 3D to select.

NRM Bill of Quantities

Grouped by NRM1 element with numbered items, rates, a collection, and cost per m² per item, bill or element.

Substructure

Excavation, working space, earthwork support, strip and pad footings, stone strips, ground beams and backfill.

Rebar schedules

Several bar sets per column, beam, wall and footing, with links, hooks and laps. Weights come out bar by bar.

Bill of materials

Cement bags, sand, aggregate, blocks, steel and boards, grouped by bill, by floor or by element.

Rate analysis

Build rates from labour, materials and plant in your own data bank. Import prices from CSV and spot missed items.

Save & share

Work is saved automatically in your browser. Save a project file to keep a copy, move to another computer or send it to a colleague.

Bill of Quantities

A priced bill you can hand over

Every item is numbered by NRM element and linked to the elements it measures. Click "show in model" to see them highlighted.

  • Type over rates or build them up in a rate analysis
  • Optional cost per m² of GFA, or of any area you choose
  • Add missed items from the item library, or check for missing ones
  • Excel export keeps live formulas, a summary and all the schedules
3D model

Check the take-off by looking at it

Every element has its own colour on the plan and in 3D, and you can change it. Hide the roof, isolate the frame, and walk the levels.

  • Structural grid lines with bubbles on every level
  • Curved walls, beams and footings, measured along the arc
  • Select many elements with a box and edit them together
Documents

Cover page, schedules and branding

Branded cover pages with a cost chart, in your company colours, for the Bill of Quantities or the bill of materials.

  • Bar schedule with shape, number, length and weight
  • Room finishes schedule: floors, skirting, walls, ceilings
  • Report styles and colour presets for your brand
How it works

From drawing to priced bill in three steps

No install and no training course needed. If you can read a drawing, you can use Takeoff Studio.

Set up the drawing

Import a PDF or image for each level, calibrate the scale from one known dimension, and lay out the grid lines by typing the bay sizes.

Trace or model

Pick a tool, such as walls, columns, slabs or rooms, and click. Enter rebar and finishes as you go. Quantities update instantly.

Price and export

Review the bill, adjust or analyse rates, add a cover page and export to Excel. Share the live project with your team.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, it's free while in beta, and you don't need an account. Open the app and start measuring.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on any laptop or desktop. Your work is saved automatically in that browser.

Which measurement standards does it follow?

Bills are grouped by NRM1 elements and measured following NRM2 rules, e.g. voids of 1 m² or less are not deducted, and curved work is kept separate with its radius stated. The rates provided are indicative. Replace them with your own or build them in the rate analysis.

Which currencies can I use?

EUR, RWF, KES, UGX, TZS, USD, GBP, ZAR and NGN, with a rate factor to adapt the built-in rates to your market.

Can my team work on the same project?

For now, share work with Project → Save project file: your colleague opens the file in their own browser. Online team projects, where everyone edits the same model live, are coming soon.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser, on your computer, plus any project files you save. Clearing your browser data removes the autosave, so save a project file for anything important.
